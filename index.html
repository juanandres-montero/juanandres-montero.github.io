About Me

Professional Profile

I am an Economist with a deep focus on the intersection of Finance, Technology, and Research. Currently working as a Research Associate at Boston Consulting Group (BCG), I specialize in the Financial Institutions practice, acting as a consultant to streamline knowledge processes.

My expertise goes beyond traditional analysis; I leverage Generative AI and Automation tools (N8N, Power Automate, Python) to build internal products and automated workflows. I actively curate a newsletter on GenAI trends and apply them to real-world banking challenges.

Previously, I analyzed Latin American housing trends at BANHVI. My background includes robust econometric modeling, full-stack web concepts (React), and advanced dashboarding.
